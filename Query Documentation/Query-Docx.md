# eSpark-HiTech-App

## E-Commerece:

1. How many screens/modules will we have?
2. In how many languages will our site be available?
3. Which theme will we follow?
4. What will be the method of shipping & payment?
5. Do you only want an eCommerce or multipurpose site?
6. Do you sell your products/services internationally?
7. Which payment gateway would you like to use?
8. Do you accept multiple currencies or just a single one?
9. Which shipping provider would you like to use?
10. What ERP system and version would you like to use?
11. Do you have developer documentation for that ERP system and version?
12. Do you have a plan on how to measure if the eCommerce project is successful or not? If yes then how will you measure it?
13. What is your scalability? What are you going to add in future?
14. What analytics solution are you using?
15. How will you manage the Admin panel & is it a separate project or not?
16. How will you get data through API? (Have you made a postman collection or API collection?)
17. Are there any events that are influencing that timeframe?
18. What is your expected timeframe for the delivery of this project?

## Marketing:

1. Do you have a digital marketing agency, or do you have an in-house marketing team?
2. What types of marketing activities do you do?
3. On what platforms do you run your digital marketing campaigns?

## Product:

1. What are you selling? What type of product are you going to sell on your site?
2. Who will provide us the data & how?
3. Why would someone buy your product/service?
4. What is your best product line?
5. What is your most recognizable and most profitable product/service?
6. Which products (product lines) are your best-sellers?
7. How many products/SKUs do you have?
8. Is the price listed or will your customers be quoted on request?
9. Do you have professional photos of your products?
10. Do you have videos of your products/services?
11. Do you required vendors to add/sell their own products?

## Customer:

1. Who is your ideal customer?
2. What customer represents the majority of your customer base?
3. What type of customers you don’t really want (but have them now)?
4. Who is the most profitable customer?
5. Do you have a competitor locally or internationally?
6. Are there any features where you want to emulate your competitors?
